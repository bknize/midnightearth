# Rules (ruleset v2)

This folder is the canon for ruleset v2: one vocabulary, one resolution chart and a new card
set, written as design, not yet built. It follows the spec [[specs/ruleset-v2/spec]]. The
guides in [[systems/systems-moc]] describe the game as it runs today and stay true until the
engine is changed to match this canon. Ben's scratch sheet is [[cards/Cards]]; the cards and
Crew were drawn from it, and it is not canon.

## Reading order

1. [[rules/glossary]] - the words, the retired words, the statuses and the trigger moments.
   Every later note is written against it.
2. [[rules/rules-v2]] - the Match, Round and Turn, Energy, Hand, Deck and Pile, card anatomy,
   statuses and movement, with what changed from today.
3. [[rules/resolution]] - the step-by-step chart of a Turn, the queue and Budget, and the
   worked traces.
4. [[rules/crew-v2]] - the ten Crew.
5. [[rules/cards-v2]] - the 90 cards, their strategy plans and the Power guideline.
6. [[rules/test-decks]] - six 40-card test decks, fixtures and not canon.

Also: [[specs/ruleset-v2/spec]] for the requirements, [[cards/Cards]] for the scratch sheet and
[[systems/systems-moc]] for the as-built guides.
